# Abyssal Waggle (`deep-sea-waggle`)

**Five Studies in Bathypelagic Bioluminescence · Specimens · Trench · Biolumen · The Vent · Shoal**

An interactive 3D X-ray radiograph visualization built with [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) (`r128`), modeled after the structure and aesthetic of [thtbee/waggles/bees-waggle](https://github.com/thtbee/waggles/tree/main/bees-waggle) and reimagined with authentic **Deep Sea Creatures**.

## Creatures & 3D Anatomy

- **Black Seadevil Anglerfish (*Melanocetus johnsonii*)**: Globular bathypelagic body with an internal X-ray vertebral column, 14 pairs of curved pleural ribs, gill arches, wide-open hinged lower jaw with 24 inward-curving translucent needle fangs, sweeping rayed caudal tail fin, and an arching dorsal illicium spine tipped with a glowing bioluminescent **Esca** lure.
- **Crown & Atolla Medusae (*Atolla wyvillei* & *Periphylla periphylla*)**: Hemispherical fluted umbrella bell with radial canals, 4 horseshoe gastrovascular gonad rings, manubrium, 4 twisted ruffled spiral oral arms, and 28 long trailing marginal tentacles animated with a traveling sinusoidal wave and rhythmic bell contraction.
- **Vampire Squid (*Vampyroteuthis infernalis*)**: Conical muscular mantle with an internal chitinous gladius pen, paired paddle-like ear fins, large camera eyes with crystalline lenses, 8 radial webbed arms with suckers and glowing arm-tip photophores, and 2 retractile velar filaments.
- **Chambered Nautilus (*Nautilus pompilius*)**: Logarithmic spiral shell with 30 internal X-ray concave septa chamber walls, central siphuncle tube, and cirrate tentacles.
- **Hydrothermal Black Smoker Vents & *Riftia* Tube Worms**: Craggy hydrothermal sulfide chimneys encrusted with giant tube worms, woven *Euplectella* glass-sponge silica lattices, and a rising hydrothermal mineral plume.

## Five Scenes

1. **01 / SPECIMEN (`0:00`)** — *Three Deep-Sea Medusae · And One Prowling Anglerfish*: Close-up X-ray radiograph of three pulsing bioluminescent jellyfish bells with trailing tentacles while a Black Seadevil anglerfish swims a figure-eight patrol with its glowing Esca lure.
2. **02 / TRENCH (`0:15`)** — *The Bathypelagic Drift · Medusae, Nautilus & Anglerfish*: A wide trench ecosystem drifting with Crown Medusae, Atolla Jellyfish, Chambered Nautilus shells, and prowling Anglerfish above hydrothermal vents and glass sponges.
3. **03 / BIOLUMEN (`0:30`)** — *Bioluminescent Wave · And The Esca Lure*: The anglerfish hovers before the hero medusa and sweeps its bioluminescent Esca lure in a figure-8 arc, triggering concentric Luciferin shockwave rings and a cascading pelagic marine-snow current.
4. **04 / THE VENT (`0:45`)** — *Hydrothermal Black Smokers · And Bioluminescent Signaling*: Five towering hydrothermal black smoker chimneys encrusted with *Riftia* tube worms and rising mineral plumes, where Vampire Squids and Anglerfish signal in the dark.
5. **05 / SHOAL (`1:00`)** — *The Pelagic Shoal · Seven Hundred Fifty Squid As One*: 750 streamlined mesopelagic squid with undulating trailing tentacles morphing through five oceanic formations (*Vortex*, *Medusa*, *Nautilus*, *Siphonophore*, and *Bathysphere*).

## Seven Radiograph Film Stocks

Switch film stocks using the top-right selector or number keys `1`–`7`:
1. **Ivory** (Warm bone plate)
2. **Cyanotype** (Prussian blue deep-sea plate — default)
3. **Sepia** (Albumen print)
4. **Silver** (Gelatin dry plate)
5. **Phosphor** (Green sonar fluoroscope)
6. **Amber** (Warm bathysphere sodium plate)
7. **Invert** (Dark abyssal negative)
